# Shotlab Privacy Policy
Effective November 5, 2026

Shotlab is a basketball video-analysis app maintained by [geepadoodle](https://github.com/geepadoodle).

## Videos and analysis
You choose which videos to import using Apple's system photo picker. Shotlab analyzes those clips on your device with Apple Vision. We do not upload your videos, body landmarks, or analysis reports to a server, and we do not use them to train models. Shotlab does not identify people or perform facial recognition.

Saved clips and reports are stored locally inside the app and excluded from iCloud backup. Delete a session in History to remove its report and saved clip. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Temporary imports and exports may remain in the app's temporary storage until iOS clears them or the app is uninstalled.

## Purchases
Apple handles subscription purchases, billing, refunds and payment details. Shotlab reads verified StoreKit entitlement information to determine whether Pro access is active. We do not receive your payment card information. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

## Tracking and sharing
The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. If you choose to share an annotated video, the destination you select receives that file and its own privacy rules apply.

## Support
Support uses [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/geepadoodle/shotlab-support/issues). Posts there are public. Do not post private videos, payment details or other sensitive information. GitHub processes account and usage information under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes and contact
Policy updates will be dated here. For privacy questions, open an issue titled “Privacy question” without including private information, or use the repository's maintainer contact options.
