# Shotlab Support
Native iPhone tools for reviewing your basketball jump shot.

[Privacy policy](privacy.md) · [Get help or report a problem](https://github.com/geepadoodle/shotlab-support/issues) · [Apple standard terms of use](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/)

## Recording
Import a 0.5–20 second video under 250 MB. Film one player from the shooting-arm side, around chest height. Include feet, hands, preparation, release and follow-through. Use bright lighting and 60 fps where available.

## Release timing and ratings
Automatic timing estimates initial arm extension, not ball separation. Scrub to the actual release and select **Set release here**. The three available signals are arm extension, torso alignment and follow-through. Scores are experimental 2D heuristics, not a validated skill ranking or prediction of shooting accuracy. Camera angle and occlusion can affect every measurement.

## Troubleshooting
- No tracking: get closer, improve lighting, keep a single player and the whole body visible.
- Unsupported clip: export a short H.264 or HEVC video from Photos.
- Missing purchase options: try reloading with a network connection. Products must be available in your storefront.
- Export: keep the app open until export completes, then use the Share button.

When reporting a bug, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce. **GitHub Issues are public; do not attach private footage.**
